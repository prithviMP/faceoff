# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome beginners and people with different backgrounds.
Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Report concerns privately to repository maintainers through a private contact channel listed on their profiles; do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers should respond fairly and protect the privacy of participants.
